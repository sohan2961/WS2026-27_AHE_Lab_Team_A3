# Evidence data

The CSV here is a **blank/partly seeded research template**, not a validated benchmark data set. Enter reported results only after opening the original paper, checking what the measurement means, and recording the exact paper page or figure.

We distinguish:
- `Measured on physical device` (experimental hardware result)
- `Post-synthesis estimate` (not a measured deployed system)
- `Derived calculation` (transparent assumptions and formula)
- `Vendor peak specification` (not achieved application performance)

Avoid averaging speedups from different models or baselines; those ratios are not a single matched benchmark.
